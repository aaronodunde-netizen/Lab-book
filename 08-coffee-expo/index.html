Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo

The Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF) is hosting the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026. The expo brings together farmers, exporters, and buyers to meet and talk about the coffee trade.

Visitors can learn about farming, quality, and export markets. Exhibitors will show their coffee and services during the three days at Speke Resort Munyonyo.
