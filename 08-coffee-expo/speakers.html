Speakers

Dr. Sarah Namukasa

Director of Coffee Development, Uganda Coffee Development Authority

Dr. Namukasa leads training that helps farmers improve harvest and post-harvest handling. She works with district teams across Uganda to raise the quality of coffee sold at home and abroad.

James Okwir

Export Manager, Great Lakes Coffee Co.

James Okwir links Ugandan suppliers with buyers in other countries. He helps cooperatives meet the paperwork and quality rules that export markets ask for.

Aisha Nalwoga

Lead Agronomist, National Coffee Research Institute

Aisha Nalwoga studies coffee soils, pests, and new varieties. Her research helps farmers choose practices that keep yields steady when the weather changes.

Peter Mugisha

Chairperson, Uganda Coffee Farmers Alliance

Peter Mugisha supports farmer cooperatives and speaks for smallholders. He works on fair prices, shared processing, and stronger farmer groups.

Call for Papers

Authors may send an abstract of 250 to 300 words. Topics include farming, trade, quality, and climate.

Send the abstract by email to papers@coffeeexpo.ug. Include the paper title, your name, and your organisation in the message.
